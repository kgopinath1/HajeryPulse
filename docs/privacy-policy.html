Hajery Pulse – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Mohammed Naser Al-Hajery & Sons Ltd ("Hajery Group", "we", "our" or "us") provides the Hajery Pulse mobile app (the "App"). The App is an internal business tool for Hajery Group employees and authorised users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal data when you use the App, in accordance with the laws of the State of Kuwait.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account information

You sign in to the App with your Hajery Group Microsoft account. When you do, we receive the following from Microsoft:

  • Full name
  • Work email address
  • Your Microsoft account identifier

We never see or store your password. Sign-in is handled entirely by Microsoft.

b. Device information

To make sure only your activated device can access company information, we collect:

  • A security key created when you activate the App with a code from IT, stored in your device's secure storage
  • Device platform (iOS or Android), device model, and app details (user agent)
  • IP address

c. Usage information

For security and audit purposes, we record which parts of the App you access and when.

d. Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint

If you choose to unlock the App with biometrics, the check is performed entirely by your device's operating system. We never receive or store your biometric data.

The App does not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment information, and it does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only to:

  • Sign you in and confirm you are authorised to use the App
  • Show you the business information you are permitted to see
  • Activate, manage and, where necessary, turn off the device you use the App on
  • Detect and prevent unauthorised access or misuse
  • Maintain security and audit records
  • Provide support when you contact us
  • Comply with Kuwaiti legal and regulatory requirements

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Microsoft, which provides the sign-in service
  • IT and hosting providers who operate the App's systems on our behalf
  • Legal or regulatory authorities where required under Kuwaiti law

All third parties are contractually obligated to safeguard your data.

4. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your personal data. All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted, access requires a Hajery Group account and a device activated with a single-use code from IT, and sign-in tokens and device keys are stored in your device's secure storage. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no online transmission is completely secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our policies. Device and audit records may be kept after your access ends, for security and compliance purposes.

6. Your Rights

In accordance with applicable Kuwaiti laws, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and regulatory requirements. You can stop using the App at any time by signing out and uninstalling it.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended only for Hajery Group employees and authorised users, and not for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
